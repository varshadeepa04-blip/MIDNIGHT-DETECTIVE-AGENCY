# User Guide: How to Play

**Goal:** work out who committed the crime, then prove it.

## The six steps (shown in the bar at the top)
1. **Cover:** move your mouse (or finger) over the black bar to reveal the hidden line. Press *Open the case files*.
2. **Case files:** choose a case. Each folder shows its difficulty, theme and your best stars.
3. **Briefing:** read the story. Press *Start investigating*.
4. **Investigate:** use the tabs.
   - Location tabs (e.g. Study, Galley): click an item to examine it.
   - **Interrogate:** every suspect has three questions. Ask as many as your actions allow.
   - Each click costs **1 action**. Easy cases are unlimited and give hints. Medium and hard cases limit your actions, so choose wisely.
5. **Evidence board:** all clues you found appear as notes.
   - Click a clue note (it highlights), then click a suspect's name to tie a **red string**. Click the same pair again to remove it.
   - Press **Accuse** on the person you believe did it.
6. **Verdict:** a stamp tells you if you were right. You get stars, a score and the full solution.

## Difficulty
| Level | Actions | Hints | Red herrings |
|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | unlimited | yes | few |
| Medium | 15 | no | some |
| Hard | 12 | no | many, plus a misleading alibi |

## Scoring (max 100)
- Wrong accusation: 0.
- Right accusation: 50 base, +10 for each key clue you tied to the culprit (max 4), -10 for each irrelevant clue tied to them, + up to 10 for unspent actions.
- Stars: 1 for solving, 2 for 60+, 3 for 85+. Ranks: Rookie, Detective, Inspector, Chief Inspector.
- Best stars are saved in your browser.

## Tips
- Alibis can be checked against physical clues.
- Someone who lies about a small detail is worth a closer look.
- Not every suspicious clue matters. Tie strings only to evidence that truly points at your suspect.
